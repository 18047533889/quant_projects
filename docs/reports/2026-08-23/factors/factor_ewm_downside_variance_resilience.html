依赖列: close

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) downside = ewm(min(ret,0)^2, span=24)
  2. 2) total = ewm(ret^2, span=24)
  3. 3) share = downside / total
  4. 4) factor = cs_rank(-share)
