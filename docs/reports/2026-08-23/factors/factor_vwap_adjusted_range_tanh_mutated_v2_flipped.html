设计意图： Mutate the parent by lengthening ATR and VWAP windows from 10 to 14 for a more robust volatility estimate, and increasing volume EMA from 20 to 30 to reduce noise in volume confirmation. This targets improved IC while preserving the core trend-volume confirmation mechanism and RankIC stability. The tanh transformation is retained to maintain rank consistency.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）   VWAP 调整振幅 (tanh v2, 翻转)

-cs_rank(tanh(ts_atr(14, 14) / vwap_30 * log(volume / ts_mean(volume, 30))))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) atr = ts_atr(high, low, close, 14)
  2. 2) ts_atr(14, 14) 14 日 ATR 二次平滑 (变异)
  3. 3) vwap_30 = ts_mean(vwap, 30)
  4. 4) vol_norm = log(volume / ts_mean(volume, 30)) 30 日量比
  5. 5) raw = atr / vwap_30 * vol_norm
  6. 6) tanh(raw) 压缩极端值
  7. 7) 已加负号翻转
说明：用 tanh 把信号压到 (-1, 1); IC<0 → 翻转。
