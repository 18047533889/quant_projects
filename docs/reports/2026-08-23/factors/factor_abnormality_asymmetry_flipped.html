设计意图： Combines volume abnormality (z-score of log volume over 20 days weighted by range ratio) with lagged volume asymmetry (ratio of up-day to down-day volume EWMA over 10 days). The product amplifies the asymmetry signal when volume is abnormal, capturing extreme volatility events with delayed volume adjustment to price direction. 因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）   异常不对称 (翻转, IC<0)

-cs_rank(abs(zscore(log(volume), 20)) * (high - low) / close * ewma_up_vol / ewma_down_vol)
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) log_vol = log(volume)
  2. 2) z_log_vol = (log_vol - ts_mean(log_vol, 20)) / ts_std(log_vol, 20)
  3. 3) abs(z) 异常量能
  4. 4) range_pct = (high - low) / close
  5. 5) ewma_up_vol / ewma_down_vol 上下行波动比
  6. 6) 原 IC<0, 已取负号翻转
说明：已加负号。原 IC<0 表示原方向排名低者后续表现好, 翻转后取正表示越异常越占优 (反向因子)。
