设计意图： Measures crash precursor via wide daily range relative to close, amplified by abnormal volume. Regime breakdown often accompanied by sudden volatility expansion and elevated participation.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）   日内振幅量比 (翻转, IC<0)

-cs_rank((high - low) / close * (volume / ts_mean(volume, 20)))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) range_ratio = (high - low) / close 当日振幅占比
  2. 2) vol_ratio = volume / ts_mean(volume, 20) 量比
  3. 3) raw = range_ratio * vol_ratio 振幅量比复合
  4. 4) 已加负号翻转 (原 IC<0)
说明：含义: 高振幅 + 放量 = 恐慌交易事件, 短期反转 IC<0, 翻转后正向选股。字段: ts_mean(volume, 20) 见 OP_DOC。
