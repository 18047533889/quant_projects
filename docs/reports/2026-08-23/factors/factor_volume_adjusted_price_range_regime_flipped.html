设计意图： Preserves the primary parent's price-volume coherence mechanism but adds a regime-dependent attenuation based on residual volatility. When residual volatility is high, the volume-adjusted range is more informative and is amplified; when low, it is damped. This targets a potential weakness in capturing volatility-regime dynamics, improving robustness without altering the core economic idea.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）   量能调整振幅 (regime, 翻转)

-cs_rank((high - low) / close * log(volume) * ts_std(20, ret))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) score = range_pct * log(volume)
  2. 2) ts_std(20, ret) 20 日波动率权重
  3. 3) factor = score * vol_regime
  4. 4) 已加负号翻转
说明：用波动率作 regime 权重; IC<0 → 翻转。
