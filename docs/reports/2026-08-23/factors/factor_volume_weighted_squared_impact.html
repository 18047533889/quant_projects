设计意图： Volume-weighted squared return amplifies larger price movements, capturing the intensity of price impact per unit of volume more strongly than absolute return. This mutation increases tail sensitivity while preserving the parent's core volume-weighted mechanism.

因子路由 / 公式   成交量加权平方冲击 (放大版)

cs_rank(amount_weighted_mean(close.pct_change()^2, amount))
依赖列: close_price, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) ret = close.pct_change(); ret2 = ret^2
  2. 2) 用 amount 加权对 ret2 求时间平均
  3. 3) factor = cs_rank(...)
说明：含义: 资金参与度高的波动越大 → 风险定价信号。
