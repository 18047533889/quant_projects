设计意图： Mutated from factor_price_impact_5d by replacing mean with median for robustness and using log volume to reduce scale sensitivity. This preserves the core price impact hypothesis while targeting the bottleneck of low mutual information and improving rank stability.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）   5 日稳定价格冲击 (翻转)

-cs_rank(ema(span=5, sign(ret) * log(volume)))
依赖列: close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) ret = 日收益
  2. 2) signed_volume = sign(ret) * log(volume) 资金方向
  3. 3) smoothed = ema(span=5, ...) 5 日稳定
  4. 4) 已加负号翻转
说明：5 日均已平滑掉单日噪声; 反向选股 (取负)。
