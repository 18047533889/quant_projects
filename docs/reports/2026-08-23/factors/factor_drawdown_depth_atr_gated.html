设计意图： Crossover of two qualified drawdown-based factors. Primary mechanism: drawdown depth normalized by ATR (63-day) from drawdown_depth_atr. Lightweight modifier: regime gating from drawdown_stress_ratio_gated that dampens the signal by 50% during high residual volatility to reduce noise. The cross-sectional rank transform enhances comparability across assets as relative drawdown severity is economica

因子路由 / 公式   回撤深度 (ATR 门控)

where(high_resvol, normalized * 0.5, normalized)
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume, style_gate_resvol_high

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) 计算滚动 N 日 close 高点 peak
  2. 2) depth = (peak - close) / peak 回撤深度 (0~1)
  3. 3) atr = ts_atr(high, low, close, 14) ATR 平均真实波幅
  4. 4) normalized = depth / (atr / close) ATR 归一化 (波动调整后)
  5. 5) 高波动期 (high_resvol=True): 乘 0.5 (半权重)
  6. 6) 低波动期: 原始 normalized
  7. 7) factor = cs_rank(...)
说明：含义: 用 ATR 把绝对回撤除掉市场波动, 只看个股相对回撤。门控逻辑: 高波动期信号不可靠, 折半权重。
