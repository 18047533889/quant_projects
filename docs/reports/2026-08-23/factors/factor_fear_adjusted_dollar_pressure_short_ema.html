设计意图： Reduced EMA span from 21 to 10 to increase responsiveness of dollar pressure signal, aiming to improve IC while preserving the core RankIC strength from the parent's regime-adjusted dollar pressure mechanism. The ATR normalization and rank transformation remain unchanged.

因子路由 / 公式   恐慌调整的美元压力 (短期 EMA)

cs_rank(-ema(span=8, abs(ret) * log(volume)))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) ret = 日收益
  2. 2) abs_ret = |ret| 收益绝对值 (恐慌代理)
  3. 3) dollar_pressure = abs_ret * log(volume) 美元压力 = 恐慌 × 资金
  4. 4) smoothed = ema(span=8, dollar_pressure)
  5. 5) 取负: 压力越低反向越稳
  6. 6) factor = cs_rank(-smoothed)
说明：含义: 短期 EMA 把单日尖刺平滑掉; 负号意味着压力低的标的占优。
