设计意图： Measures the normalized imbalance between downside and upside squared return shocks across overnight and intraday sessions. A 30-session rolling average smooths transient shocks, while a bounded EMA30 volume ratio provides lightweight participation confirmation without changing the core session-asymmetry mechanism.

因子路由 / 公式   盘间不对称平滑确认 (30 日)

cs_rank(ema(span=15, sign(ret) * log(volume)) * ts_std(30, ret))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) signed_vol = sign(ret) * log(volume)
  2. 2) smoothed = ema(span=15, signed_vol)
  3. 3) vol_regime = ts_std(30, ret)
  4. 4) score = smoothed * vol_regime
  5. 5) factor = cs_rank(score)
说明：盘间量价方向 × 30 日 regime, IC 为正 (未翻转)。
