设计意图： Mutate parent factor_return_change_smoothness by replacing rolling mean with EWMA for faster response, flipping direction so smoother price action (lower jitter) predicts continuation, and using continuous bounded volume scaling. Targets Price-Volatility Behavior layer: low return jitter confirmed by active volume indicates conviction.

因子路由 / 公式   EWMA 平滑度 + 量能裁剪

cs_rank(-ema(span=10, abs(ret - ts_delay(ret, 1))) * clip(vol_ratio, 0.5, 1.5))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) ret = 日收益
  2. 2) jerk = abs(ret - ts_delay(ret, 1)) 单日收益的二阶差 (急动度)
  3. 3) jerk_ema = ema(span=10, jerk) 急动度的 EWMA
  4. 4) vol_ratio = volume / ts_mean(volume, 20) → clip 到 [0.5, 1.5]
  5. 5) 取负: jerk 越小越平滑 → 排名靠前
  6. 6) factor = -jerk_ema * clip_vol_ratio
说明：已显式带负号 (负 jerk × 缩放量比 → 越平滑越靠前)。
