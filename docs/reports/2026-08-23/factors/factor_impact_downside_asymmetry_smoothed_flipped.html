设计意图： Mutates the parent's liquidity-sensitive downside-impact asymmetry by removing the unstable volume-correlation modifier and extending the impact baseline and signal smoothing from 20 to 30 bars. This preserves the core economic mechanism while directly targeting the parent's weak predictive robustness and excessive turnover/payoff failure through a lower-noise, slower-moving signal. 因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）   下行冲击不对称 (平滑, 翻转)

-cs_rank(ema(span=10, where(ret < 0, abs(ret), 0) * log(volume)))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) down_ret = where(ret < 0, abs(ret), 0) 只取下行幅度
  2. 2) score = down_ret * log(volume) 下行资金冲击
  3. 3) smoothed = ema(span=10, score)
  4. 4) 已加负号翻转
说明：含义: 下行 + 放量越大越差, IC<0 表示该信号反向有效 (取负号)。
