1. Factor 总分公式

FactorScore = 0.25·Predictive + 0.15·Stratification + 0.15·Portfolio + 0.15·Stability + 0.10·Robustness + 0.10·Novelty + 0.05·Tradability + 0.05·StatisticalReliability
