EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

Overall Assessment: GOOD
- Most functions have basic empty array and NaN handling

5. No condition number checks for ill-conditioned matrices

    Current: epsilon=1e-6 prevents infinity, but weight can be ~1e6
