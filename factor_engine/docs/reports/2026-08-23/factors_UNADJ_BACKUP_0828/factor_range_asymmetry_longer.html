设计意图： Captures asymmetric volatility by comparing average intraday range on downtrend days versus uptrend days. Using a longer 60-day window produces a more stable estimate of the leverage effect, reducing noise while preserving the core economic mechanism.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

ratio = ts_mean(high-low | down) / ts_mean(high-low | up) over 60 days
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume
