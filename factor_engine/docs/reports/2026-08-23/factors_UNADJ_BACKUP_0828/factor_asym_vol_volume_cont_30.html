设计意图： Continuous volume scaling replaces hard threshold to capture finer participation dynamics, enhancing nonlinear interaction while preserving asymmetric volatility as core risk signal. Targets improved IC and MI, maintains RankIC stability.

因子路由 / 公式

multiply(log(divide(ts_std(abs(minimum(ts_pct(close, 1), 0.0)), 30), ts_std(maximum(ts_pct(close, 1), 0.0), 30))), divide(volume, ts_mean(volume, 20)))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) ret = close.pct_change()
  2. 2) up_returns = max(ret,0)；down_returns = min(ret,0)
  3. 3) up_vol = ts_std(30, up_returns)；down_vol = ts_std(30, |down_returns|)
  4. 4) asym = log(down_vol / up_vol)
  5. 5) vol_ratio = volume / ts_mean(volume, 20)
  6. 6) factor = asym * vol_ratio
