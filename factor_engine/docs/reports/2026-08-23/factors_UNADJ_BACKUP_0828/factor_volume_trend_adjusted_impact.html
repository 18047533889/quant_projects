设计意图： This factor mutates the parent volume-adjusted price impact by replacing the sqrt(volume) denominator with a volume trend ratio (volume / 20-day moving average). This emphasizes price moves that occur on abnormally high volume relative to recent trend, capturing conviction-driven movements. The EWM smoothing improves stability and consistency, targeting the IC bottleneck while preserving the paren
