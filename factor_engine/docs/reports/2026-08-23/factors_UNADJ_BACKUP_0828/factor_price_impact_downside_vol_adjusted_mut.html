设计意图： Mutation of the parent crossover factor. The parent combined price impact per unit volatility with a downside/upside return magnitude ratio, but failed the payoff gate on top quantile excess return. To address this, I simplified the modifier: changed the asymmetry window from 20 to 60 for more stable estimates, and replaced multiplicative scaling with a dampener that reduces the signal when upside

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

factor = ewm_mean(20, |ret|/vol) / EWM_std(ret, 20) * dampener, dampener = 1 if ratio>=1 else ratio, ratio = -ts_sum(60, neg_ret)/ts_sum(60, pos_ret)
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume
