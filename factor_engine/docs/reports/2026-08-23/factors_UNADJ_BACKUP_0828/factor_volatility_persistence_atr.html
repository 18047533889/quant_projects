设计意图： Mutation of parent factor_return_volatility_persistence. Replaces rolling return std with ATR to measure price volatility more broadly using the high-low range, then smooths with EMA to capture persistence. This targets improved robustness and predictive power while preserving the core mechanism of low volatility persistence.
