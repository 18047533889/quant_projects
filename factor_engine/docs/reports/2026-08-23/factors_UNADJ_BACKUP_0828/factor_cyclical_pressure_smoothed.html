设计意图： Uses an exponential moving average of true range (EMA-90) instead of simple average to capture recent volatility changes more responsively while preserving the cyclical pressure interpretation. The 20-day rate of change of EMA-ATR remains the core signal.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-(ATR_EMA90(20d change) = (ewm_mean(90, TR) / ts_delay(20, ewm_mean(90, TR))) - 1)
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume
