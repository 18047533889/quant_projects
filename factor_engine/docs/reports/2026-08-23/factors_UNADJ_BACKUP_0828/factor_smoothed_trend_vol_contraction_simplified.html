设计意图： Simplification of parent: removed the signed square root transformation on the contraction term and added a clip to [-1,1] to bound extreme values. This preserves the core trend-contraction interaction while reducing unnecessary nonlinearity, potentially improving rank stability and interpretability. Keeps cross_sectional_transform as none to maintain raw payoff mechanism.
