设计意图： Preserves the regime-adaptive drawdown oversold-reversion mechanism but uses a longer ATR window (20 vs 14) to provide smoother volatility normalization, reducing noise in the drawdown/volatility ratio. This targets improving IC and ICIR while maintaining the parent's RankIC stability. Cross-sectional rank ensures cross-stock comparability.
