设计意图： Mutation: replaced SMA(21) with EMA(21) for dollar imbalance smoothing to reduce lag and improve IC while preserving the core fear-adjusted pressure mechanism and strong RankIC/RankICIR. Single core mechanism, no modifiers.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-(subtract(multiply(rank(divide(multiply(ts_ema(multiply(subtract(close, open), volume), span=21), add(1.0, divide(subtract(ts_delay(close, 1), close), ts_delay(close, 1)))), ATR_WILDER(high, low, close, 21))), 2.0), 1.0))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) dollar_imb = (close-open)*volume
  2. 2) smoothed = ts_ema(21, dollar_imb)
  3. 3) price_decline = (ts_delay(close,1)-close)/ts_delay(close,1)
  4. 4) fear_adj = smoothed * (1+price_decline)
  5. 5) raw = fear_adj / ATR_WILDER(high, low, close, 21)
  6. 6) factor = rank(raw)*2-1
