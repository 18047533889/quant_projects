设计意图： Mutates parent factor_nonlinear_deviation by scaling the close deviation from SMA20 by rolling volatility of returns. This adjusts for market regime changes, potentially improving IC and mutual information while preserving the bounded nonlinearity and rank stability from the parent. The hyperbolic tangent compresses outliers.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

tanh((close - SMA20(close,20)) / (volatility(close,20) * SMA20(close,20)))
