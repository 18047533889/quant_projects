设计意图： Directional pressure is inferred by combining the magnitude of price movement (from previous close to high for buying, to low for selling) with volume confirmation. The net volume-weighted movement over a trailing window reflects buying minus selling pressure. Smoothing reduces noise. This mechanism uses high, low, and previous close together with volume, a new raw information pathway.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-(((high - prev_close)*volume - (prev_close - low)*volume).(20).ts_sum() / volume.(20).ts_sum(), smoothed by 10-day EMA)
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume
