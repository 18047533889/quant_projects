设计意图： Crossover of asymmetric volatility (parent 1) with relative range (parent 2) to enhance detection of informed selling pressure. The primary mechanism captures downside vs upside volatility asymmetry with volume confirmation over 60 days. The borrowed lightweight idea adds the relative intraday range (high-low)/close, amplifying the signal when the range is wide, consistent with informed liquidity

因子路由 / 公式

multiply(multiply(subtract(log(add(1.0, sqrt(ts_mean(power(minimum(ts_pct(close, 1), 0.0), 2), 60)))), log(add(1.0, sqrt(ts_mean(power(maximum(ts_pct(close, 1), 0.0), 2), 60))))), divide(volume, ts_mean(volume, 60))), divide(subtract(high, low), close))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) ret = close.pct_change()
  2. 2) down_var = ts_mean(60, min(ret,0)^2)；up_var = ts_mean(60, max(ret,0)^2)
  3. 3) down_vol = sqrt(down_var)；up_vol = sqrt(up_var)
  4. 4) asym = log(1+down_vol) - log(1+up_vol)
  5. 5) vol_ratio = volume / ts_mean(volume, 60)
  6. 6) rel_range = (high-low)/close
  7. 7) factor = asym * vol_ratio * rel_range
