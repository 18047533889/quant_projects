设计意图： This factor preserves the volatility-regime switching intuition of the parent by comparing 20-day rolling volatility to its 60-day median, but replaces the raw 10-day price change with a smoothed exponential moving average (EMA) of close. The EMA reduces noise and improves rank stability, targeting the RankIC bottleneck while maintaining the core regime-dependent momentum/mean-reversion hypothesis

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

regime = sign(vol_20 - med_vol_60); factor = regime * (close - ewm_mean(10, close))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume
