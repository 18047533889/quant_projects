设计意图： Sales yield (1/ps_ttm) is a stable value metric. Multiplying by inattention (1 - rank of free_turn) amplifies the value effect when attention is low. The parent had strong RankIC/ICIR but weaker IC; winsorizing the product at rolling 5th/95th percentiles reduces extreme values that harm linear correlation while preserving rank ordering and the economic mechanism.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

1/ps_ttm * (1 - ts_rank(free_turn, 20)), then rolling 60-day winsorize at 5% and 95% quantiles to reduce outlier drag on IC.
依赖列: ps_ttm, free_turn
