设计意图： Mutation of factor_volume_adaptive_momentum_smoothed_v3: reduce volume regime window from 20 to 10 for more responsive volume adaptation, and correspondingly reduce EMA span from 15 to 10 to align smoothing with faster volume signal. This targets improved IC by reacting more quickly to volume changes, while preserving the core volume-adaptive momentum strength from the parent.

因子路由 / 公式

-(ts_ema(multiply(subtract(divide(close, ts_delay(close, 5)), 1.0), log(clip(MYDSL("classify_volume_regime", volume, 10, vol_ratio), 0.1, 10.0))), span=10))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) vol_ratio = classify_volume_regime(volume,10).vol_ratio [alpha_tools]
  2. 2) ret5 = close/ts_delay(close,5)-1
  3. 3) vol_weight = log(clip(vol_ratio, 0.1, 10))
  4. 4) factor = ts_ema(10, ret5*vol_weight)
