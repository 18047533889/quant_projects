设计意图： Mutation from parent factor_cheapness_leverage_adjusted: replace earnings yield (1/PE) with book-value cheapness (1/PB) to use a more stable valuation anchor. The leverage penalty from debt is preserved. This changes the raw cheapness field from profit-based to book-based, targeting the low raw IC caused by earnings volatility while keeping the same economic penalty structure.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持
