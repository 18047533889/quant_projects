设计意图： Uses a long-term rolling mean (200 days) to capture the central cycle level, but scales the deviation by short-term volatility (20-day rolling std) to adapt to changing market conditions. This produces a more robust oversold/overbought signal: a stock far from its long-term average is only extreme if recent volatility is low, reducing false signals during high-volatility periods.
