设计意图： Time-series size context: when a stock's market cap is relatively large compared to its own history, earnings yield is more reliable; when relatively small, book yield adjusted for leverage avoids distress risk. This uses mkt_cap_float as a time-series conditioner, not a cross-sectional amplifier.
