设计意图： Negative skew accumulation: rolling 20-day skewness of log returns (inverted to positive when skew is negative) combined with daily range/close to weight by intraday volatility. Smoothed with a 10-day EMA. Negative skew signals left-tail risk accumulation, a distinct tail-risk mechanism in the Extreme Risk & Fragility layer.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-ts_skew(20, log_return) * (high-low)/close, smoothed ewm_mean(10)
依赖列: open, high, low, close
