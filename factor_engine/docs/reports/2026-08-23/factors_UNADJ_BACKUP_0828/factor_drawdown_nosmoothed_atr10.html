设计意图： Simplified drawdown/ATR factor by removing the SMA smoothing and shortening ATR window from 14 to 10 to increase responsiveness and potentially improve IC, while preserving the core regime-adaptive trailing high mechanism for drawdown calculation. Cross-sectional rank ensures cross-stock comparability.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

drawdown = (regime_adaptive_trailing_high(40/60) - close) / regime_adaptive_trailing_high; atr = ts_atr(10); raw = drawdown / atr
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume, style_gate_resvol_high
