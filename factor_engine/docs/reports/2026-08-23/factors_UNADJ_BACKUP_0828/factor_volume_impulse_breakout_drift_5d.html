Logic: For each day, compute the positive distance above the prior 20-day high "kw">class="kw">and scale it by the positive volume deviation "kw">class="kw">from the 20-day mean. Average this product over the last 5 days "kw">class="kw">and divide by the cumulative volume impulse over the same window, yielding the average post-impulse breakout progress. Sub-mechanism tags: volume_impulse, delayed_adjustment, breakout_drift, price_volume_confirmation.
