因子路由 / 公式

-(where(gt(style_gate_resvol_high, 0.0), multiply(ts_ema(multiply(subtract(high, low), divide(volume, ts_ema(volume, span=10))), span=10), 0.9), multiply(ts_ema(multiply(subtract(high, low), divide(volume, ts_ema(volume, span=10))), span=10), 1.0)))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume, style_gate_resvol_high

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) volume_ema = ts_ema(10, volume)
  2. 2) raw = (high-low) * volume/volume_ema
  3. 3) smoothed = ts_ema(10, raw)
  4. 4) factor = where(high_resvol>0, smoothed*0.9, smoothed)
