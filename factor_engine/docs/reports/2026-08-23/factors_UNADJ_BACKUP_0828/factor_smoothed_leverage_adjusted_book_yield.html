设计意图： Crossover: retains the stable book yield cheapness mechanism (leverage-adjusted book yield) from the primary qualified parent, and borrows the smoothing technique (4-period rolling mean) from the secondary parent to reduce noise. Smoothing improves signal stability without altering the core value hypothesis. Post-transform cs_rank preserves relative cheapness interpretability.
