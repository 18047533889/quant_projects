设计意图： Preserves the core mechanism of lagged return amplified by log volume ratio and volatility adjustment. Adds a lightweight regime adaptation: in high residual volatility states (style_gate_resvol_high), a longer EMA smoothing (10 days) reduces noise; otherwise, the original EMA5 preserves responsiveness. This targets improved stability in turbulent periods without weakening the parent's strength.

因子路由 / 公式

-(where(gt(style_gate_resvol_high, 0.0), ts_ema(multiply(multiply(subtract(divide(close, ts_delay(close, 5)), 1.0), log(divide(volume, ts_ema(volume, span=30)))), divide(ATR_WILDER(high, low, close, 20), close)), span=10), ts_ema(multiply(multiply(subtract(divide(close, ts_delay(close, 5)), 1.0), log(divide(volume, ts_ema(volume, span=30)))), divide(ATR_WILDER(high, low, close, 20), close)), span=5)))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume, style_gate_resvol_high

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) ret_5 = close/ts_delay(close,5)-1
  2. 2) ema_vol = ts_ema(30, volume)
  3. 3) log_vol_ratio = log(volume/ema_vol)
  4. 4) atr_20 = ATR_WILDER(high, low, close, 20)
  5. 5) raw = ret_5 * log_vol_ratio * (atr_20/close)
  6. 6) factor = where(high_resvol>0, ts_ema(10,raw), ts_ema(5,raw))
