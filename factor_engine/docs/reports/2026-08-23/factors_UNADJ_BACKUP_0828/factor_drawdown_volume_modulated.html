设计意图： Preserves the rank-normalized drawdown geometry strength (high RankICIR) while introducing a multiplicative modulation with volume ratio to amplify signal when trading activity confirms drawdown structure. This targets improvement in IC and MI by capturing nonlinear volume-price interactions without sacrificing rank stability.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-(multiply(divide(add(add(rank(divide(subtract(ts_max(close, 252), close), ts_max(close, 252))), rank(duration)), rank(-(ts_delta(divide(subtract(ts_max(close, 252), close), ts_max(close, 252)), 5)))), 3.0), add(1.0, rank(divide(volume, ts_mean(volume, 20))))))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) rolling_high = ts_max(close, 252)
  2. 2) drawdown = (rolling_high-close)/rolling_high
  3. 3) recovery = -drawdown.diff(5)
  4. 4) vol_ratio = volume/ts_mean(20,volume)
  5. 5) score = (rank(dd)+rank(duration)+rank(rec))/3
  6. 6) factor = score * (1+rank(vol_ratio))
