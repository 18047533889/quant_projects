设计意图： This Price-Volatility Behavior signal measures tail fragility rather than ordinary return volatility. A stock in a sustained drawdown whose recent intraday excursions are disproportionately below the prior close has asymmetric downside volatility and weaker shock absorption; the negative sign favors assets with less such fragility. Squared tails emphasize economically consequential downside excurs

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-(20-day downside tail-energy share) × (60-day close drawdown), where tail energy is squared excursion from prior close to low or high.
依赖列: high, low, close

"kw">class="kw">def factor_tail_fragility_drawdown_asymmetry(df): """Drawdown-conditioned asymmetry of downside versus upside intraday tail excursions.""" df_copy = df.copy() previous_close = df_copy["close"].shift(1) downside_tail = (previous_close - df_copy["low"]).clip(lower=0.0) / previous_close.replace(0.0, np.nan) upside_tail = (df_copy["high"] - previous_close).clip(lower=0.0) / previous_close.replace(0.0, np.nan) downside_energy = downside_tail.pow(2).rolling(20, min_periods=20).sum() upside_energy = upside_tail.pow(2).rolling(20, min_periods=20).sum() tail_asymmetry = downside_energy / (downside_energy + upside_energy).replace(0.0, np.nan) drawdown = 1.0 - df_copy["close"] / df_copy["close"].rolling(60, min_periods=60).max().replace(0.0, np.nan) df_copy["factor_tail_fragility_drawdown_asymmetry"] = -tail_asymmetry * drawdown "kw">class="kw">return df_copy["factor_tail_fragility_drawdown_asymmetry"]
