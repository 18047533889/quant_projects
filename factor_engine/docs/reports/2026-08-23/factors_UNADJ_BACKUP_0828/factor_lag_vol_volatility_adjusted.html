设计意图： Captures delayed adjustment between volatility, volume, and returns. Lagged return (5-day) is amplified by high relative volume (log volume ratio) and high volatility (ATR/close), then smoothed with EMA5 to reduce noise. The product expresses how past return signals strengthen when both volume and volatility are elevated, reflecting slower information diffusion.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-(ewm_mean(5, (close/ts_delay(close, 5)-1) * log(volume/ewm_mean(volume, 30)) * (ATR20/close)))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume
