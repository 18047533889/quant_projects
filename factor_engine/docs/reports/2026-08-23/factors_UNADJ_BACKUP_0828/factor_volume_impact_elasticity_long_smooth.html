设计意图： Measures the rolling price-response elasticity of participation: the 60-session covariance between close-to-close returns and log volume divided by the rolling variance of log volume. The longer window smooths transient covariance estimates, while the variance floor avoids unstable ratios during near-constant-volume periods.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-(Cov_60(return, log(volume)) / Var_60(log(volume)) when Var_60(log(volume)) > 1e-4, otherwise NaN)
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

"kw">class="kw">def factor_volume_impact_elasticity_long_smooth(df): """Estimate a smoother rolling price-response slope to changing participation.""" df_copy = df.copy() ret = df_copy["close"].pct_change().replace([np.inf, -np.inf], np.nan) log_volume = np.log(df_copy["volume"].clip(lower=1.0)) covariance = ret.rolling(60, min_periods=60).cov(log_volume) volume_variance = log_volume.rolling(60, min_periods=60).var(ddof=1) stable_variance = volume_variance.where(volume_variance > 1e-4) factor = covariance.div(stable_variance) factor = factor.replace([np.inf, -np.inf], np.nan) df_copy["factor_volume_impact_elasticity_long_smooth"] = factor "kw">class="kw">return df_copy["factor_volume_impact_elasticity_long_smooth"]
