设计意图： Preserves the core volume-weighted intraday momentum mechanism from parent. Adds a lightweight volume regime adjustment: on low-volume days (detected by classify_volume_regime), the signal is halved to reduce noise from thin trading. The EMA(10) smoothing is retained for stability. This targets improving RankIC and RankICIR by filtering out low-quality observations.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-(pressure = ((close - open) / open) * volume; smoothed = ewm_mean(10, pressure); halve on low-volume days)
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume
