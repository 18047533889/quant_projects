Logic: The breakout resistance is built directly as the trailing 20-day high of high shifted three bars, so a single series represents the fixed resistance "kw">class="kw">for the breakout bar "kw">class="kw">and the two hold bars. The confirmed-breakout mask multiplies the percentage hold distance by the breakout-day close location "kw">class="kw">and the breakout-day above-median volume flag. This preserves the parent mechanism "kw">class="kw">while removing redundant shift operations on the prior-high series. Sub-mechanism tags: breakout, hold-confirmation, close-location, volume-confirmation.
