设计意图： This factor combines smooth directional consistency (from parent stability factor) with crash risk measured by negative skewness (from crash skew parent). By downweighting the stability signal when crash risk is high, it aims to preserve the strength of the stability factor during normal regimes while protecting against false trends during extreme risk periods. The rank normalization of crash skew
