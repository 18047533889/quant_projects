设计意图： Drawdown depth normalized by recent volatility (ATR/close) isolates the magnitude of price decline relative to typical daily movement, improving cross-sectional comparability without artificial ranking. The 60-day window captures intermediate-term stress, while 20-day ATR provides a responsive volatility scaling. No cross-sectional transform is needed as the ratio naturally scales across stocks.

"kw">class="kw">def factor_drawdown_stress_atr(df): df_copy = df.copy() roll_max = df_copy["close"].rolling(60, min_periods=60).max() drawdown = (roll_max - df_copy["close"]) / roll_max.replace(0.0, np.nan) atr = pd.Series(talib.ATR(df_copy["high"].astype(np.float64).values, df_copy["low"].astype(np.float64).values, df_copy["close"].astype(np.float64).values, timeperiod=20), index=df_copy.index) atr_ratio = atr / df_copy["close"].replace(0.0, np.nan) factor = drawdown / atr_ratio.replace(0.0, np.nan) factor.name = "factor_drawdown_stress_atr" "kw">class="kw">return factor
