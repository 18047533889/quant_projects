设计意图： Preserves the primary drawdown stress mechanism (depth * occupancy) from parent1, which captures acute price pressure from multi-session drawdowns. Adds a lightweight volatility attenuation from parent2: scaling stress by 1/(1+20d rolling std of daily returns). This reduces false signals during high-volatility periods, a predictive weakness of the raw stress signal. The economic intuition is that
