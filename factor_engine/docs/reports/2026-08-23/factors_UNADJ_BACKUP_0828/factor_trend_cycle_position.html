设计意图： Long-term trend cycle position: Measures distance of price from 200-day SMA normalized by 20-day ATR. High positive values indicate overextended uptrend, negative values indicate oversold downtrend, capturing cyclical mean-reversion in market phases. Cross-sectional ranking allows comparison of deviation across stocks.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-((close - ts_mean(200, close)) / ts_atr(20))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

输入字段
