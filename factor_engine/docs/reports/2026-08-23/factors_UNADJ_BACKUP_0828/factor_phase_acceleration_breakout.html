设计意图： This factor targets a market-phase transition mechanism: long-term uptrends become more valuable when the slow trend is accelerating and price is breaking above the slow average. It is not a generic momentum stack; the signal emphasizes expansion out of a neutral phase into a sustained cycle upswing, which is characteristic of the Market Structure & Cycle layer.
