设计意图： Crossover borrows the primary mean reversion mechanism (smoothed intraday close position) from mutation_001 and adds a lightweight volume confirmation ratio from mutated_stability_002. The volume ratio amplifies the contrarian signal when volume is above its 20-day average, aiming to improve cross-sectional rank stability (RankIC/IR) while preserving the IC and ICIR strength of the mean reversion

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-ts_mean(5, (close-midpoint)/(high-low)) * (volume / ts_mean(20, volume))
