Preserves size-gated value mechanism but adds smoothing (5-day rolling mean) to cash flow yield and sales yield to reduce noise and improve the raw linear correlation (IC). The leverage penalty on small-cap sales yield is retained. Cross-sectional ranking remains to ensure comparability. Parent strength in RankIC and RankICIR is preserved.
