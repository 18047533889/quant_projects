设计意图： Mutation of crossover_001_g1: removed the 5-period smoothing on the intraday position fraction to make the reversal signal more responsive to current bar overreaction. The direct deviation from 0.5 captures immediate intraday mean-reversion, while the volume rank (percentile of volume ratio over 20 days) filters noisy signals. This simplification targets improved ICIR and RankICIR while preserving

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

rev * vol_rank where rev = -((close-low)/(high-low) - 0.5) and vol_rank = ts_rank(20, volume / rolling_mean(volume,20))
