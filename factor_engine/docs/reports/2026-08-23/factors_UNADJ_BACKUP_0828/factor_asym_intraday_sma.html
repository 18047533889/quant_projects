设计意图： Simplifies the intraday asymmetry measure by using simple moving averages instead of exponential, reducing sensitivity to recent outliers and improving stability. Window increased to 40 for a more persistent asymmetry signal.

因子路由 / 公式

divide(ts_mean(subtract(open, low), 40), ts_mean(subtract(high, open), 40))
依赖列: open, high, low

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) upside = high - open；downside = open - low
  2. 2) sma_up = ts_mean(40, upside)；sma_down = ts_mean(40, downside)
  3. 3) factor = sma_down / sma_up 下行/上行振幅比
