设计意图： Synergy fusion: rolling correlation between price and volume rates of change measures alignment. When price and volume move together (high corr), short-term trend is favored; otherwise, long-term trend dominates. This continuous weighting creates an adaptive trend signal that exploits orthogonal information from two trend horizons and two data streams (price and volume), targeting balanced metric

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持
