设计意图： Combines book-value cheapness with financial health (low leverage) to capture value stocks with lower distress risk. The leverage penalty aims to improve linear predictive power (IC) by reducing exposure to overleveraged value traps that often perform poorly.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

cheapness = 1/pb_lf, health = 1 - debttoassets/100, composite = cheapness * health
依赖列: pb_lf, debttoassets
