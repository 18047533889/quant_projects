设计意图： Measures the depth of volume dry-up (5-day vs 60-day volume shortfall) together with close-range compression (10-day vs 60-day). Extreme dryness inside a tight range indicates absorption capacity / seller exhaustion; the raw signal is stock-specific, and same-day cross-sectional rank makes relative dryness comparable.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

dry = 1 - ts_mean(5, vol)/ts_mean(60, vol); tight = 1 - Rng(close,10)/Rng(close,60); signal = dry * tight
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume
