设计意图： Net volume-weighted directional movement (buying minus selling pressure) is the primary mechanism. A continuous ADX weight from the cyclic trend confidence parent attenuates the signal in weak-trending markets, reducing false directional pressure signals and targeting the payoff weakness of the volume-pressure parent.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-(((high - prev_close)*volume - (prev_close - low)*volume).(20).ts_sum() / volume.(20).ts_sum() * (ts_adx(50)/100), smoothed by 10-day EMA)
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume
