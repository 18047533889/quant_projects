设计意图： This factor combines a volatility-adjusted price impact measure (primary) with a volume tail asymmetry adjustment (lightweight modifier). The primary mechanism captures liquidity costs per unit volatility. The asymmetry modifier amplifies the signal when selling pressure is dominant (down volume > up volume relative to VWAP) and dampens it when buying pressure is dominant, as volume imbalance can

ts_mean(|ret|/vol) / ts_std(ret) * (1 + volume_tail_asymmetry)
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume
