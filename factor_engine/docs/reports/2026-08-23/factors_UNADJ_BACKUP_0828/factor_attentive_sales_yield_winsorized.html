设计意图： Mutation of attentive sales yield: added rolling winsorization (5th/95th percentile over 60-day window) to the product of sales yield and inattention. This reduces extreme values that weaken linear correlation (IC) while preserving the strong rank ordering (RankIC, RankICIR) and nonlinear dependence (MI). The core mechanism remains: value (sales yield) amplified when turnover is low (inattention).
