设计意图： Volume momentum (short-term vs long-term volume SMA) smoothed with exponential weighting to reduce noise. Volume acceleration/deceleration captures changing investor interest; smoothing improves signal stability while preserving the core trend-following mechanism. Borrows EWM smoothing from the intraday volatility skew parent to reduce turnover and false signals.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-(ts_mean(10, volume)/ts_mean(30, volume) - 1, then ewm_mean(span=5))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume
