设计意图： Measures drawdown severity normalized by close rolling standard deviation instead of ATR. Close volatility is less influenced by intraday extremes, reducing noise in the stress ratio. This targets the parent's payoff bottleneck (negative top quantile excess return) while preserving the core drawdown/volatility intuition. Cross-sectional cs_rank remains to highlight relative stress across stocks.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

stress_ratio = -(close / ts_max(20) - 1) / close_std(20)
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume
