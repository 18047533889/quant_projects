设计意图： Combines book-value yield with leverage penalty and mid-cap size tilt from the primary parent, and adds a ROA quality multiplier from the secondary parent. Firms with higher ROA get a stronger value signal, while negative ROA firms are penalized. This provides a quality confirmation to avoid distressed value traps beyond leverage alone.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

book_yield = 1/pb_lf; leverage_adj = (1 - debttoassets/100) clipped [0.1,1]; size_w = 4*percentile_rank*(1-percentile_rank) clipped [0.1,1]; roa_conf = (1 + roa2_ttm2/100) clipped [0.5,2]; signal = book_yield * leverage_adj * size_w * roa_conf
依赖列: pb_lf, debttoassets, mkt_cap_float, roa2_ttm2
