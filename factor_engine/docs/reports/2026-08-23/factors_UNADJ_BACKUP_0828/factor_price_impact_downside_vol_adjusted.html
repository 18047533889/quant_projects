设计意图： This factor combines the primary price impact per unit volatility mechanism (payoff-first) with a lightweight downside/upside return magnitude ratio modifier from a predictive-first parent. The modifier amplifies the impact signal when negative returns have been larger in magnitude than positive returns over a 20-day window, addressing the primary parent's symmetry weakness and targeting improved
