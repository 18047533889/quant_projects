设计意图： This factor focuses on where closes settle relative to the broken 20-day high rather than on the breakout move itself. A close far above the old boundary relative to that day's trading range signals strong acceptance of the new level, while a close hugging the boundary signals a weak hold. Averaging over five days makes the signal a durable settlement premium.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-(ts_mean(5, I(close > res20) * (close - res20) / (high - low)))
依赖列: high, low, close
