设计意图： This factor measures how consistently a stock has stayed above its prior 20-day high over the trailing 5 days. Persistent holds are a sign that the upper bound has been repriced rather than merely touched, while a breakout that immediately falls back receives a low score. The small excess amplification keeps the count signal continuous and rewards holds with meaningful follow-through.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-(persistence = sum_{i=0..4} I(close_{t-i} > prior_high_{t-i}); factor = persistence * (1 + 2*max(close/prior_high - 1, 0)))
依赖列: high, close
