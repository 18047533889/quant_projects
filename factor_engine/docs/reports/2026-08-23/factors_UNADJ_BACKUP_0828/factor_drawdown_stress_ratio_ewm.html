设计意图： Mutates the parent by replacing the rolling sum of drawdown with an exponential weighted moving average, giving more weight to recent drawdowns. This smooths the stress signal and improves signal stability and ICIR/RankICIR while preserving the core economic intuition: tail stress accumulation relative to volatility.
