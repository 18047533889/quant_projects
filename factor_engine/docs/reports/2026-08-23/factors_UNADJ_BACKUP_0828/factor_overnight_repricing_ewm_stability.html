设计意图： This preserves the parent’s overnight-versus-intraday price-discovery mechanism while replacing a hard 63-day cutoff with exponentially weighted smoothing. Recent repricing leadership receives more weight, while gradual decay of older observations should reduce window-boundary instability and target stronger IC consistency without adding a second signal family.

因子路由 / 公式

divide(subtract(ts_ema(MYDSL("decompose_overnight_intraday", close, open, overnight_ret), span=63), ts_ema(MYDSL("decompose_overnight_intraday", close, open, intraday_ret), span=63)), ts_ema(add(abs(MYDSL("decompose_overnight_intraday", close, open, overnight_ret)), abs(MYDSL("decompose_overnight_intraday", close, open, intraday_ret))), span=63))
依赖列: open, close

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) (overnight_ret, intraday_ret) = decompose_overnight_intraday(close, open) [alpha_tools 自命名 DSL]
  2. 2) overnight_ewm = ts_ema(63, overnight_ret)
  3. 3) intraday_ewm = ts_ema(63, intraday_ret)
  4. 4) activity_ewm = ts_ema(63, |overnight_ret|+|intraday_ret|)
  5. 5) factor = (overnight_ewm - intraday_ewm) / activity_ewm
