因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）

where(gt(style_gate_resvol_high, 0.0), multiply(ts_ema(multiply(subtract(high, low), divide(volume, ts_ema(volume, span=20))), span=15), 1.1), multiply(ts_ema(multiply(subtract(high, low), divide(volume, ts_ema(volume, span=20))), span=15), 0.9))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) volume_ema = ts_ema(20, volume)
  2. 2) raw = (high-low) * volume/volume_ema
  3. 3) smoothed = ts_ema(15, raw)
  4. 4) factor = where(resvol_high>0, smoothed*1.1, smoothed*0.9)
