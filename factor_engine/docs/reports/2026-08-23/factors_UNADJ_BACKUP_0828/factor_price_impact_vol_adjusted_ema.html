设计意图： This factor simplifies the parent's volatility-adjusted price impact by removing the volume imbalance asymmetry modifier, which could introduce noise. It uses exponential weighting to smooth the impact and volatility estimates, reducing lag and potentially improving RankIC and IC while preserving the low-turnover payoff profile. The core economic mechanism remains: high price impact per unit volum
