设计意图： This factor captures drawdown depth (distance from trailing high), recovery geometry (rate of drawdown decrease), and market roughness (rolling std of returns). The product of these three components amplifies mean-reverting signals when drawdown is deep, recovery is accelerating, and noise is high, reflecting multi-scale complexity in drawdown dynamics.
