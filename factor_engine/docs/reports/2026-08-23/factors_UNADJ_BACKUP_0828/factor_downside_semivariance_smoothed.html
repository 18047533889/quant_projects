设计意图： This mutation preserves the parent’s lower-tail fragility mechanism: it measures the share of recent realized variance attributable to losses. A short causal exponential smoothing layer is added to reduce transient day-to-day shifts in the bounded concentration estimate, targeting more stable cross-sectional ordering and lower signal churn without introducing another economic feature family.

因子路由 / 公式

ts_ema(divide(ts_mean(power(minimum(ts_pct(close, 1), 0.0), 2), 20), ts_mean(power(ts_pct(close, 1), 2), 20)), span=5)
依赖列: close

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) ret = close.pct_change() 当日收益
  2. 2) downside_sq = ts_mean(20, min(ret,0)^2) 20日下行方差
  3. 3) total_sq = ts_mean(20, ret^2) 20日总方差
  4. 4) concentration = downside_sq / total_sq 下行方差占比
  5. 5) factor = ts_ema(concentration, span=5) 指数平滑
