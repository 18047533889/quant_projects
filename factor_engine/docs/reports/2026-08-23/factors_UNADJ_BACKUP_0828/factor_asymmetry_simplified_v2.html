设计意图： Shortened rolling window to 20 days for more responsive asymmetry measurement and reduced smoothing to 3 days to preserve faster signals while reducing noise. Core mechanism remains the log ratio of downside to upside semi-deviation, capturing the leverage effect. Cross-sectional rank ensures comparability across assets.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

F = log(σ_down / σ_up) where σ_down = sqrt(mean of squared negative returns over 20 days), σ_up = sqrt(mean of squared positive returns over 20 days), smoothed with 3-day MA.
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume
