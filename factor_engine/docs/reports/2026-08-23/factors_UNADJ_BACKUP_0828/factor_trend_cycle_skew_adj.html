设计意图： Combines the primary trend cycle position (payoff-first parent) with a lightweight tail risk attenuation from the predictive-first parent: when recent returns exhibit negative skew (left-tail risk accumulation), the factor is scaled down by 20% to avoid overexposure to stocks with deteriorating risk profiles, targeting improved top-quantile payoff stability.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-((close - ts_mean(200, close)) / ts_atr(20) * (1 - 0.2 * (ts_skew(20, log_return) < 0)))
