设计意图： Combines drawdown depth normalized by ATR with a volatility expansion ratio as an adaptive weight. During volatile market phases (high ATR expansion), the drawdown signal is amplified, reflecting faster mean-reversion expectations. The cross-sectional rank is retained to capture relative severity across stocks.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

depth = (close / ts_max(63, close) - 1) * -1; normalized = depth / ts_mean(63, TR); exp_ratio = ts_mean(20, TR) / ts_mean(120, TR); factor = normalized * clip(exp_ratio, 0.5, 2.0)
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume
