设计意图： Measures drawdown severity normalized by average true range instead of close volatility. ATR captures intraday and gap volatility, providing a more robust stress metric that may improve cross-sectional ranking and long-only payoff by reducing noise from close-only volatility.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

stress_ratio = -(close / ts_max(20) - 1) / ts_atr(20)
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume
