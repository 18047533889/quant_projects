设计意图： Combines volume abnormality (z-score of log volume over 20 days weighted by range ratio) with lagged volume asymmetry (ratio of up-day to down-day volume EWMA over 10 days). The product amplifies the asymmetry signal when volume is abnormal, capturing extreme volatility events with delayed volume adjustment to price direction. 因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）

multiply(multiply(abs(divide(subtract(log(add(volume, 1.0)), ts_mean(log(add(volume, 1.0)), 20)), ts_std(log(add(volume, 1.0)), 20))), divide(subtract(high, low), close)), where(gt(ts_ema(where(not_(ge(close, open)), volume, 0.0), span=10), 0.0), divide(ts_ema(where(ge(close, open), volume, 0.0), span=10), ts_ema(where(not_(ge(close, open)), volume, 0.0), span=10)), 1.0))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) log_vol = log(volume+1)
  2. 2) z = (log_vol - ts_mean(20,log_vol)) / ts_std(20,log_vol)
  3. 3) abnormality = |z| 异常量能
  4. 4) range_ratio = (high-low)/close
  5. 5) vol_abnorm = abnormality * range_ratio
  6. 6) up_day = close>=open；vol_up=volume where up，vol_down=volume where down
  7. 7) ewma_up = ts_ema(10, vol_up)；ewma_down = ts_ema(10, vol_down)
  8. 8) ratio = where(ewma_down>0, ewma_up/ewma_down, 1.0)
  9. 9) factor = vol_abnorm * ratio
