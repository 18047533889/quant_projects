设计意图： This factor captures long-term market phase using the difference between 50-day and 200-day EMAs, normalized by price. It adjusts for volatility regime by downweighting periods of high ATR, assuming cyclical transitions between trending and volatile phases. Cross-sectional ranking isolates stocks in the strongest trending phase.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-((EMA50 - EMA200) / Close * (1 - ATR14 / Close))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume
