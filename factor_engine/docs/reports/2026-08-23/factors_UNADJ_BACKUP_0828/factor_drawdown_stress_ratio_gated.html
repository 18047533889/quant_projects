设计意图： Drawdown stress ratio (accumulated drawdown depth relative to volatility) adaptively dampened during high residual volatility periods to reduce noise and improve ranking stability. The primary mechanism from the drawdown stress parent is preserved; the gate from the volatility adaptive trend parent is added as a lightweight modifier.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

raw = cumulative_max_drawdown_sum_60d / 60d_vol; result = raw * (1 - 0.5 * I(style_gate_resvol_high > 0))
