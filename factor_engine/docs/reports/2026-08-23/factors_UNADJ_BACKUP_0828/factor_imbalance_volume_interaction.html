设计意图： This factor multiplies cumulative order imbalance (directional pressure) by a volume abnormality signal (z-score times normalized range). Order imbalance alone captures buying/selling pressure, but its predictive power is enhanced when volume is abnormal and price range is wide, indicating informed trading. The product amplifies signals during high conviction periods while reducing noise during no

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

OI * (vol_z * norm_range), where OI = sum_{i=t-19}^{t}(CLV_i * volume_i), CLV = (2*close - high - low)/(high - low), vol_z = (volume - rolling_mean_20)/rolling_std_20, norm_range = (high - low)/close
依赖列: high, low, close, volume
