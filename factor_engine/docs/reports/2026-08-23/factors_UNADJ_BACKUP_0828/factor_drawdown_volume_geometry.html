设计意图： Extends the multi-scale drawdown geometry by incorporating volume confirmation. Depth is multiplied by normalized volume to emphasize drawdowns with heavy participation, while duration and recovery change remain additive. This nonlinear interaction targets the mutual information bottleneck without disrupting rank stability.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

add(add(multiply(rank(divide(subtract(ts_max(close, 5000), close), ts_max(close, 5000))), rank(divide(volume, ts_mean(volume, 20)))), rank(duration)), rank(ts_delta(divide(subtract(ts_max(close, 5000), close), ts_max(close, 5000)), 10)))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) running_max = close.expanding().max() → ts_max(close, 5000) 近似（全历史）
  2. 2) depth = (running_max - close) / running_max
  3. 3) duration = 自新高以来的天数（事件分组 cumcount）
  4. 4) depth_chg = depth.diff(10) → ts_delta(depth, 10)
  5. 5) vol_ratio = volume / ts_mean(volume, 20)
  6. 6) factor = rank(depth)*rank(vol_ratio) + rank(duration) + rank(depth_chg)
