Large caps benefit from cash flow yield boosted by ROE weighted by float scarcity: low-float high-ROE large caps have greater re-rating potential. Small caps retain book yield with leverage penalty. Cross-sectional rank ensures cross-size comparability.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

For large caps: (1/pcf_ocf_ttm) * (1 + max(0, roe_ttm2/100) * (1/log(1+free_float_shares))). For small caps: (1/pb_lf) * max(0, 1 - debttoassets/100). Default large_val for medium caps.
