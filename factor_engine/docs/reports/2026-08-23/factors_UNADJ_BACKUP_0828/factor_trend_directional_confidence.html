设计意图： Long-term trend cycle position weighted by directional efficiency. The trend deviation from SMA200 normalized by ATR20 captures overextended/oversold conditions. Multiplying by directional efficiency (median of absolute close return / intraday range over 10 days) amplifies the signal when price moves are directionally strong and attenuates it on noisy, range-bound days. -((close - ts_mean(200, close)) / ts_atr(20) * ts_median(10, |close_pct_change| / (high-low)))
