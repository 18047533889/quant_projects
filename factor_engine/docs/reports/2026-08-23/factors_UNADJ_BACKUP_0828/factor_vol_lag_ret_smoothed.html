设计意图： This factor captures the delayed adjustment between volatility, volume, and returns. Lagged 5-day return is multiplied by log volume ratio (to emphasize unusual volume) and amplified by the current volatility level (ATR/close). The EMA5 smoothing models the gradual incorporation of information into price, representing lagged response. The combination targets stable rank ordering across IC, RankIC,

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-(ewm_mean(5, (close/shift5 - 1) * log(volume/ewm_mean(volume, 20)) * (1 + ATR20/close)))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume
