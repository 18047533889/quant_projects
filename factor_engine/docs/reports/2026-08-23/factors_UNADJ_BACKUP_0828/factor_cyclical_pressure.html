设计意图： Measures cyclical state transitions by rate of change of 90-day average true range. Expanding ATR indicates increasing cyclical pressure and potential regime shift.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-(ATR90(20d change) = (ATR90 / ts_delay(20, ATR90)) - 1)
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume
