设计意图： Free-float size conditions the re-rating elasticity of book-value cheapness. Smaller free-float stocks have higher sensitivity to fundamental improvements. A debt-to-assets screen protects against financial distress. The mechanism tests whether cheapness amplified by float context and filtered by health predicts returns.
