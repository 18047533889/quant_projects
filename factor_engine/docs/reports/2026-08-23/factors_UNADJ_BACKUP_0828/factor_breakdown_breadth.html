设计意图： Crash precursor: intraday range widens relative to close-to-close volatility before a regime breakdown. This captures the shift where daily extremes expand faster than daily returns, indicating fragility and potential crash. Cross-sectional ranking selects stocks with the most extreme range/vol divergence.
