设计意图： Mutation of CROSS-001: preserves the core price-volume coherence mechanism (10-day return multiplied by volume deviation) but replaces the standard volume z-score (mean/std) with a robust z-score using median and MAD. This reduces the influence of outlier volume observations, aiming to improve IC and MI stability without disrupting the RankIC strength of the parent.
