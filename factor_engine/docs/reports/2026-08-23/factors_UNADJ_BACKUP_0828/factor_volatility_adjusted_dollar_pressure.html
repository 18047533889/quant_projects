设计意图： Volatility-adjusted dollar pressure measures directional dollar imbalance scaled by recent volatility, capturing risk-adjusted buying/selling pressure. EMA smoothing reduces noise, and ATR provides volatility normalization. The rank transform delivers a stable cross-sectional ordering suitable for balanced IC, RankIC, and MI.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-(subtract(multiply(rank(divide(ts_ema(multiply(subtract(close, open), volume), span=21), ATR_WILDER(high, low, close, 21))), 2.0), 1.0))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) dollar_imb = (close-open)*volume
  2. 2) smoothed = ts_ema(21, dollar_imb)
  3. 3) raw = smoothed / ATR_WILDER(high, low, close, 21)
  4. 4) factor = rank(raw)*2-1
