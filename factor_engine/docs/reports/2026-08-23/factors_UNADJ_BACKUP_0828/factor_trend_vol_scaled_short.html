设计意图： Shortened the EMA windows from 50/200 to 20/50 to increase responsiveness to trend changes while preserving the long-term volatility normalization. The parent's weakness in IC and RankIC suggests the slow trend signal missed shorter-term momentum shifts. By using faster EMAs, the factor captures more timely trend reversals while the 60-day volatility scaling maintains risk-adjusted comparability.
