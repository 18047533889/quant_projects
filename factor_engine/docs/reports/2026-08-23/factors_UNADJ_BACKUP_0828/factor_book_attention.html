设计意图： Mutation of earnings-yield attention factor (ACA-TURN-002) replacing trailing PE with book-to-price to mitigate negative-earnings distortion and unstable PE ratios. The inattention component from free_turn rank is preserved to retain the low-attention value premium hypothesis. Cross-sectional transform removed because raw composite outperformed transformed in the parent.

因子路由 / 公式

multiply(divide(1.0, pb_lf), subtract(1.0, ts_rank(free_turn, 20)))
依赖列: pb_lf, free_turn

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) book_yield = 1/pb_lf 便宜度
  2. 2) free_turn_rank = ts_rank(free_turn, 20) 近20日换手分位
  3. 3) inattention = 1 - free_turn_rank 不活跃度
  4. 4) factor = book_yield * inattention
