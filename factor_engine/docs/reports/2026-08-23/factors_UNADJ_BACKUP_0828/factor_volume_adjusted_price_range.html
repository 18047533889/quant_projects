设计意图： Mutation of parent: reduced smoothing window from 10 to 5 and volume EMA span from 20 to 10 to increase responsiveness of price-volume coherence signal, targeting improved IC while preserving the core economic mechanism and style gate regime adaptation.

因子路由 / 公式

-(where(gt(style_gate_resvol_high, 0.0), multiply(ts_mean(multiply(subtract(high, low), divide(volume, ts_ema(volume, span=10))), 5), 1.2), multiply(ts_mean(multiply(subtract(high, low), divide(volume, ts_ema(volume, span=10))), 5), 0.8)))
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) volume_ema = ts_ema(10, volume)
  2. 2) raw = (high-low) * volume/volume_ema
  3. 3) smoothed = ts_mean(5, raw)
  4. 4) factor = where(resvol_high>0, smoothed*1.2, smoothed*0.8)
