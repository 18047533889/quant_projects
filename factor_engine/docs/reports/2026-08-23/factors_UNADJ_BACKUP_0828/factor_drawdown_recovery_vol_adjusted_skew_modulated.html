设计意图： Drawdown recovery potential is stronger when the drawdown is accompanied by left-tail stress (negative skew), as panic selling creates deeper overshoots with higher mean-reversion probability. This crossover combines the primary drawdown/volatility mechanism with a lightweight skew modulation from the tail-stress parent.
