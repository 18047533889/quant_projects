设计意图： Mutation of factor_drawdown_stress_vol_scaled. Shortens drawdown detection window to 40 days and occupancy window to 10 days, making the signal more responsive to recent stress. Doubles the volatility attenuation coefficient to emphasize low-volatility regimes, which are more conducive to mean reversion. Targets improvement in IC and RankIC while preserving the positive payoff mechanism.
