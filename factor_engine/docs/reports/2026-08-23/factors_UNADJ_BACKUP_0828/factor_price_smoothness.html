设计意图： Measures smoothness of daily price action by comparing return magnitude to high-low range. Low ratio indicates the close is near the midpoint (choppy/smooth), high ratio indicates strong directional movement. Negative rolling average assigns high values to periods of smooth, consistent pricing, aligning with temporal stability.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-ts_mean(20, (close/ts_delay(close)-1)^2 / (high-low)^2)
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

输入字段

字段含义说明
