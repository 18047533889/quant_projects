设计意图： Simplified value-quality composite: book yield multiplied by a financial health score based on low debt. Replaces the parent's ROE change trend with debt-to-assets to improve raw IC stability and avoid noise from volatile profitability trends. The raw signal is used without cross-sectional transform to target linear correlation improvement.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

composite = (1/pb_lf) * max(0, 1 - debttoassets/100)
依赖列: pb_lf, debttoassets
