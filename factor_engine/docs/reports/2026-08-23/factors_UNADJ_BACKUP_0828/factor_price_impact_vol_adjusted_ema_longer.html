设计意图： Extended the EMA span from 20 to 40 for both impact and volatility estimates to capture longer-term liquidity cost and return volatility trends. This smoother measure may improve predictive stability (ICIR, RankICIR) while preserving the core mechanism: high price impact per unit volume normalized by volatility signals low liquidity. Cross-sectional rank remains for comparability across stocks.
