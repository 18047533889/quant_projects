设计意图： Mutates factor_volume_adaptive_momentum_smoothed by adding a trailing 20-day volatility normalization to the 5-day return. This preserves the core volume-adaptive momentum and smoothing while targeting improved IC by making the return component risk-adjusted. The parent's strength in RankIC and RankICIR is preserved through the same log volume weighting and EMA smoothing.

因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）⚠ FE DSL 暂不支持

-(ewm_mean(span=10, (close/ts_delay(close,5)-1) / trailing_vol(20) * log(vol_ratio))))
