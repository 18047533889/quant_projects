设计意图： Volatility-adjusted price impact using a longer-term volatility estimate (40-day rolling std) to provide a more stable risk adjustment. This reduces noise in the factor and improves cross-sectional ranking consistency while preserving the core liquidity/impact intuition. The 20-day impact window captures recent market microstructure, while the 40-day volatility window captures persistent risk.
