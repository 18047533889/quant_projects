设计意图： Geometric & Fusion layer mechanism measuring directional consensus: a high ratio of absolute close return to intraday range indicates that most of the day's price movement occurred in one direction, consistent with herding and crowding. The rolling median smooths daily noise. Cross-sectional rank emphasizes relative efficiency across assets.
