Adds a lightweight volume regime adjustment: on low-volume days (detected by classify_volume_regime), the signal is halved to reduce noise from thin trading. The EMA(10) smoothing is retained for stability. This targets improving RankIC and RankICIR by filtering out low-quality observations.
