因子路由 / 公式 ⚠ 已翻转（IC<0）

ts_ema(multiply(subtract(high, low), divide(volume, ts_ema(volume, span=10))), span=10)
依赖列: open, high, low, close, volume

📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) volume_ema = ts_ema(10, volume)
  2. 2) raw = (high-low) * volume/volume_ema
  3. 3) factor = ts_ema(10, raw)
