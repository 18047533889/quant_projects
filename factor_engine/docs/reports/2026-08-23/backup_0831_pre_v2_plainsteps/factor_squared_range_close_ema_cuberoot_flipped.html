📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) range_sq = (high-low)^2
  2. 2) raw = range_sq / close
  3. 3) smoothed = ts_ema(10, raw)
  4. 4) factor = sign(smoothed) * |smoothed|^(1/3)
