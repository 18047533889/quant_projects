📝 计算步骤 (一步一步解释)

  1. 1) volume_ema = ts_ema(20, volume)
  2. 2) raw = (high-low) * volume/volume_ema
  3. 3) smoothed = ts_ema(10, raw)
  4. 4) factor = where(resvol_high>0, smoothed*1.1, smoothed*0.9)
